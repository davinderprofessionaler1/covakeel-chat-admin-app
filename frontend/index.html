What can CoVakeel do for you?

Behind every answer is a complete library of Indian tax law: GST, Income Tax and Customs. On top of it sits an AI that reads it for you.

Ask the way you would ask a senior colleague. Get a clear answer in simple words, not a page of search results.

CoVakeel
Can I claim GST credit on a car I bought for my business?
CoVakeel AI

Usually not. Credit is blocked on cars that seat 13 people or fewer, driver included. It is allowed if you sell such cars, carry passengers, or teach driving.

CGST Act · Section 17(5)(a)
